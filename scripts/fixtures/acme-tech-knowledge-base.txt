# Acme Tech — Company Knowledge Base

## About Us

Acme Tech is a B2B SaaS company founded in 2019, headquartered in San Francisco. We build AI-powered workflow automation tools that help mid-market companies reduce operational overhead and scale faster without increasing headcount.

Our flagship product, **FlowBot**, integrates with 150+ enterprise tools — including Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Slack, and SAP — and lets non-technical teams automate complex multi-step processes through a no-code drag-and-drop interface.

As of 2024 we serve over 3,400 customers across 42 countries, process more than 280 million automated actions per month, and maintain 99.97% uptime SLA.

## Products

### FlowBot Core
The main automation platform. Key features:
- Visual workflow builder with 400+ pre-built connectors
- Conditional logic, loops, and branching paths
- Real-time execution monitoring and error alerts
- Role-based access control and audit logs
- SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant

Pricing: $299/month (Starter, up to 5 seats), $899/month (Growth, up to 25 seats), Enterprise custom pricing.

### FlowBot AI
An AI layer on top of Core that adds:
- Natural-language workflow creation ("Build me a lead nurture sequence")
- Intelligent error recovery — the AI diagnoses failures and suggests fixes
- Anomaly detection on workflow performance metrics
- Predictive scheduling based on historical load patterns

Available as an add-on for $199/month or included in Enterprise.

### FlowBot Analytics
A reporting module that surfaces:
- Per-workflow ROI estimates (time saved × average hourly cost)
- Error rate trends and SLA breach forecasting
- Seat utilization and license optimization recommendations

Included in Growth and Enterprise plans.

## Common Customer Pain Points We Solve

- **Lead routing delays**: Sales ops teams manually assign inbound leads based on territory and product interest. FlowBot automates this in real time, cutting average lead response time from 4 hours to under 2 minutes.
- **Invoice approval bottlenecks**: Finance teams juggle email threads and spreadsheets to get invoices approved. FlowBot routes invoices automatically based on amount, vendor, and cost center, with escalation paths if SLAs are missed.
- **Onboarding fragmentation**: HR creates accounts in 8+ systems manually for every new hire. FlowBot triggers a unified onboarding workflow from a single HRIS event, provisioning all accounts within 15 minutes.
- **Customer churn signals**: CS teams miss early churn signals buried in product usage data. FlowBot monitors usage thresholds and auto-creates tasks for CSMs when accounts go dark.

## Tone and Brand Voice

Acme Tech communicates as a knowledgeable, practical partner — not a vendor. We avoid hype and buzzwords. We speak in specifics (metrics, time savings, dollar amounts). We respect the reader's intelligence and don't over-explain basics.

Content pillars:
- **Operational efficiency** — how to do more with the same team
- **Reliability and trust** — enterprise-grade infrastructure, compliance, security
- **Customer success stories** — real companies, real numbers
- **Product education** — how to use FlowBot features effectively

Avoid: generic AI hype, vague promises ("transform your business"), and technical jargon targeted at developers when the audience is operations or business.
